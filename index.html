Close your open tabs and drop your anchor in the URL bar, weary web traveler. You've navigated your way through the endless, buzzy highways of the modern internet to end up out here, past the corporate feeds, at my corner of the World Wide Web. Clear your cache, let your browser rest its connection, or run a DNS lookup over at LINKS whenever you want to surf to the next domain. Feel free to keep the session open, linger for a while, and inspect the system.

Consider this corner of cyberspace a snapshot of my personal hard drive. It is definitely not flawless: it’s noisy, held together by raw curiosity and a lot of YouTube tutorials. You can blame this whole mess on one video that sent me down the rabbit hole of personal web pages late one night. Watching someone build a site from scratch sparked an obsession, and I fell so hard that weeks of studying were traded for late night code and hijacked sleep.

Nothing on this domain is static, and the markup keeps growing with every late night push. Hidden across these digital corridors are small secrets and easter eggs [Only 2 for now]. Dig around long enough and the machine will slowly reveal to you the guy on the other side of the screen.

Keep the connection alive as long as your journey allows, and don’t forget to commit an entry to the guestbook before terminating the session.
